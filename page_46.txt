44  One Tap Delay  Time  Sets the delay time. Maximum delay time is 1480 ms.  Tempo  The Tempo parameter sets the relationship to the global Tempo e.g. if you set it to 1/4T you will get quarter note triplets of the tapped tempo. 1/4 equals the BPM (tapped Tempo). If you select “Ignored” the current parameter will use the Preset value and will not be affected by the Global Tempo.  Feedback  Adjusts the amount of Feedback.  Pan position  Controls the panning of the Delay.  Lowcut  Cuts off frequencies lower than the selected. The slope of the filter is 12dB/octave.  Highcut  Cuts off frequencies higher than the selected frequency. So if you feel that there is too much treble in your signal, you remove this by adjusting the highcut filter down. The slope of the filter is 12dB/octave.  Mix  Mix between direct sound and effect.  In Level  Controls the input level of the block.  Out Level  Controls the output level of the Delay block  Mute mode  See Mute mode in the introduction of this chapter.
