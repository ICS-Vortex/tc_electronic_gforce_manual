Out Level  Controls the output level of the block.  Mute mode  See Mute mode in the introduction of this chapter.  Formant filters  The Formant filters are somewhat special. They can create a new sound for you. Imagine a pedal that does not say WAH WAH, but instead goes OOWEE- OOWEE. Formants are also known as the vowels of human speech. The basic idea is that you set three coordinates, Start, Thru and End in a frequency area, and then move your sound up and down the curve you created with the coordinates.  FORMANT CURVE  Start  The Starting point of the formant curve.  Thru  The Thru point of the curve  End  The End point of the curve  Resonance  Sets the amount of resonance on the Formant filter.  Age/Sex  Is used to change the character of the formant curve. Try to experiment a bit with this parameter.  Sweep  Dial the Sweep parameter to move up and down the formant curve. This is the parameter that you should connect to a pedal or some other modifier. Try to connect the Sweep parameter to an LFO, ENV or an expression pedal for realtime parameter control.
